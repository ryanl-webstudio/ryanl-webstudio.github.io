How it's made

Why it's better

  • 01 Ball

    Fresh beef, loosely rolled. No frozen pre-formed patties, ever.

  • 02 Smash

    Pressed hard and thin onto a ripping-hot flat-top. That's where the flavor happens.

  • 03 Crust

    The edges go dark, crispy and lacy while the middle stays juicy.

  • 04 Melt

    Cheese goes on, gets a quick steam, then onto a toasted bun and into your hands.
