Top of the Hill is a family-owned restaurant serving old-fashioned greasy burgers, with fresh-cut vegetables and hand-pressed patties made daily. Order at the counter, grab a seat under the flags, and bring an appetite.

People plan trips around this place. A couple from Denmark once made it a planned stop on their visit to America after a friend shared one of our posts.
